## How numbers get into the paper

`paper/main_template.tex` contains tokens such as `{{P_viable:.1f}}` and `{{T_ci}}`. `code/09_build_paper.py` reads the result files, formats the values, and writes `paper/main.tex`. If you want to change the text, edit the template, not `main.tex`. If you change a script, rerun `09_build_paper.py` and the numbers in the paper update.
